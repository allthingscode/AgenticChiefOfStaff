# Health & Wellness Profile

## Dietary Framework
- **Primary Diet:** Keto (50% plant based, 50% animal based).
- **Strict Constraints:** Low-carbohydrate, strictly no added sugars. The primary goal is to avoid glucose spikes and increase health span.
- **Approved Staples & Brands:** Miracle Noodle, Hero (bread/pasta alternatives), Danger Coffee.

## Morning Routine: Cold Plunging
- **Equipment:** "Desert Plunge" cold plunge tub with a "Polar Dive" water chiller.
- **Current Protocol:** Typical routine is 45°F for about 15 minutes, 3 times per week.

## Goals & Tracking
- **Current Focus:** Finding high-protein, nutrient dense meals that fit the low-carb constraint.

## Anti-Preferences (Do NOT Suggest)
- No recipes containing high-carb vegetables (e.g., potatoes, corn).
